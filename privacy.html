Privacy policy

Atlas mail reader is used only by its owner, for the owner's own email accounts.

What it accesses

Read-only access to email in accounts whose owner signed in and gave permission. It cannot send, delete, or change email.

How the data is used

Email is copied to the owner's own private server so the owner's personal assistant software can search and summarize it for the owner. Nothing else.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared, or disclosed to anyone. No data is used for advertising or to train AI models.

Google user data

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

An account owner can remove access at any time from their Google Account under Security, Third-party apps and services. Stored copies are then deleted from the private server.
